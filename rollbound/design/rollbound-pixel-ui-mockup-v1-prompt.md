## Final prompt

Use case: ui-mockup  
Asset type: high-fidelity landscape gameplay screen mockup for the original indie game ROLLBOUND

Input images: Images 1-5 are style and mood references only. Borrow broad qualities such as crisp low-resolution pixel art, near-black backgrounds, framed HUD panels, a readable map, and high-contrast colored icons. Create an original layout and original assets; do not reproduce any reference game's characters, maps, card layouts, logos, or exact UI.

Primary request: Design one complete Rollbound gameplay screen centered on a winding board-game path made from approximately 10 visible square pixel-art tiles. The path represents a linear sequence even though it bends through a dark fantasy landscape. A small original cloaked adventurer stands on the current tile. A large pixel-art D6 has just rolled a 5. The player is evaluating three possible destinations: Nudge -1, accept 5, or Nudge +1.

Tile system: every tile has a consistent outer footprint and is a miniature environmental diorama with a unique silhouette. Combat tiles have sharp neon-red frames and cracked ground with a monster or crossed weapon motif. Camp has a neon-green frame, tiny tent and orange campfire. Treasure has a gold-yellow frame, chest and glints. Shop has a cyan frame, tiny merchant stall and hanging sign. Event has a violet frame and mysterious shrine or portal. Blank road tiles have dim slate frames and varied stones, grass, roots or ruins. Elite uses a jagged double magenta-red frame. The distant boss endpoint is larger and ominous with ivory and red framing.

Scene/backdrop: top-down three-quarter view over a near-black plum fantasy void with sparse pine trees, rocks, ruins and narrow connecting paths; the board is the dominant visual focus.

Style/medium: authentic crisp pixel-art game UI, limited palette, hard pixel edges, chunky one-pixel and two-pixel outlines, subtle stepped pixel glow only; no smooth painting, no blur, no anti-aliased vectors.

Composition/framing: 16:9 desktop game screen. Compact top HUD with hero portrait, HP and XP bars, level, damage, armor, gold, Nudge and Reroll counts. Main board occupies roughly 70 percent of the screen. Bottom decision panel shows three large destination choices aligned under the board and a prominent pixel-art die. Keep strong hierarchy and breathing room.

Interaction states: ordinary tile frames are dim. The rolled destination is brightest with a white inner outline. The two Nudge alternatives glow slightly less. One combat destination visibly shows a small skull and HP cost. Visited tiles are desaturated. The hero has a light ivory outline so it remains readable on every tile.

Color palette: background #0B0712; combat #FF3B4D; elite #FF2BD6; camp #39FF88; treasure #FFD84A; shop #25D9FF; event #A66BFF; blank #61556F; boss ivory #FFF2DF plus red. Neon is used as informational accent, not across every surface.

Constraints: visually legible at a glance; color plus icon, silhouette and frame shape must identify every tile; practical game UI rather than concept art; no tiny illegible paragraphs; no excessive bloom; no gradients; no 3D rendering; no mobile phone frame; no logos or trademarks from the references; no watermark.
